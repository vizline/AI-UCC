# Everyday Task Judgments Study

## General information for participants

You are invited to take part in an academic study conducted by Maciej Koscielniak at SWPS University, Poland. The study examines how people form judgments about ordinary situations and tasks.

### What will I be asked to do?

You will read a short description of an everyday situation, take a brief moment to imagine it in detail, and answer questions about your impressions. You will also be asked for basic demographic information and about some of your general experiences. There are no right or wrong answers; we are interested in your honest views.

The study takes approximately **8–12 minutes** and should be completed in one sitting on a device with a stable internet connection. Please read each instruction carefully and respond without consulting other people.

### Are there any risks or benefits?

No risks beyond those encountered in ordinary reflection and online questionnaire participation are anticipated. Some questions may ask you to evaluate a person or their work, but you may stop participating at any time before submitting your answers. There is no direct personal benefit from taking part, although your responses may contribute to scientific knowledge about social judgment and decision-making.

### What information will be collected?

The study records your questionnaire responses, basic demographic information, response timings, and the participant, study, and session identifiers supplied by Prolific. We do not ask for your name or email address. Your Prolific identifier is collected only to manage participation, data quality, and payment and will not be used to contact you outside Prolific.

Responses will be analysed in aggregate for academic research. Research data will be handled in accordance with applicable institutional and data-protection requirements. Please do not enter your name or other identifying information in optional comment fields.

### Participation and payment

Taking part is voluntary. You may leave the study at any point before submitting your responses without giving a reason. Payment and eligibility are administered through Prolific under the conditions shown on the study listing. After submitting the questionnaire and reading the debriefing information, you will be given a completion link that returns you to Prolific.

### Questions or concerns

If you have questions about the study, please contact the researcher, Maciej Koscielniak, at SWPS University, Poland, using the contact details provided in the Prolific study listing or through Prolific messaging.

By starting the study through Prolific, you confirm that you have read the information above and meet the eligibility requirements displayed in the study listing.
